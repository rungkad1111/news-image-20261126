A one-image front-page summary of the world news of Thursday 26 November 2026, for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261126`. The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) validates and copies the shipped `entry.png` (1024×1024) to `/output/entry.png`. The image was drawn programmatically (Pillow) as a newspaper front page: masthead, dateline, lead story with illustration, and seven briefs.

Sources: Prime Minister's Office release dated 26 November 2026; AP.

7. **Geneva — World Chess Championship, Game 3** — Defending champion Gukesh Dommaraju and challenger Javokhir Sindarov met in Game 3 on Thursday 26 November, after draws in Games 1 and 2. Sources: ChessBase India; Chess Herald (schedule: Game 2 Wed 25 Nov, Game 3 Thu).

The matches ended late on Wednesday evening, with the results carried in Thursday 26 November editions. Source: The Daily Jagran sports headlines, 26 November 2026.

## Dating notes

Items 6 and 8 straddle midnight: the Gulf crash happened on Tuesday and the Champions League matches ended late on Wednesday evening (CET), but both were first carried as confirmed news in Thursday 26 November editions; the five lead items (fire, Carney package, Moscow talks, Thanksgiving, Constitution Day) are dated 26 November itself. Stories from 24–25 November that were checked and deliberately excluded: Bangladesh garment wage (+36%, 25 Nov), North Korea's expulsion of the Swiss envoy (25 Nov), the Chen Jining graft probe (25 Nov), the FT report on shelved Russia business deals (25 Nov), ESA's Hera asteroid-crater images (25 Nov), Venezuela's $237.5bn debt reveal (24 Nov), Petrobras' $122bn plan (23 Nov), and the Cartagena supermarket rescue (25 Nov).
